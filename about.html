About Benvek

Smart air. Better living.

A solutions-focused company based in Hyderabad, India, specializing in ventilation, HVAC, central vacuum and energy-efficient building solutions.

Who we are

Practical building technology for better spaces.

According to its company booklet, Benvek provides products and practical solutions designed to improve indoor air quality, comfort, hygiene and energy efficiency for residential, commercial and industrial spaces.

Its stated product and service range includes fresh air ventilation systems, exhaust systems, commercial kitchen ventilation, central vacuum systems, HVAC systems, solar solutions and heat pump systems.

Our mission

Reliable. Efficient. Innovative.

To deliver reliable, energy-efficient and innovative building solutions that create healthier, more comfortable and sustainable environments.

Our vision

Long-term value through quality and service.

To become a trusted name in ventilation and building technology solutions by delivering quality products, excellent service and long-term value to customers.
