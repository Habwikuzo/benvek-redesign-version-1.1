Fresh Air Ventilation

Energy-recovery ventilators and decentralized heat-recovery products listed in the Benvek booklet.

Enquire about ventilation

Why it matters

Bring fresh air indoors while recovering energy.

Heat-recovery ventilation exchanges indoor and outdoor air while transferring heat through a heat exchanger. Benvek's booklet presents centralized ERV equipment and decentralized recuperators for fresh-air and exhaust operation.

System design, filtration and performance vary by model and installation requirements.
